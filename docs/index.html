DamoDemo

Polish your own recordings one layer at a time: clean up noise, tighten timing, shape the tone. Then listen to the layers together, balance them, and render the song. Adjust it in plain English.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Your audio is never uploaded. Nothing is generated: every sound is yours.

How to use DamoDemo

  1. Record your takes. One instrument or vocal per recording, all at the same tempo (a click track helps), all starting from the same moment.
    • Leave about a second of silence at the start of each take, so the noise clean-up can learn your room.
    • Tune your guitar first: tuning correction is for vocals only, and isn't in this browser version yet.
    • On iPad: record in Voice Memos or GarageBand, then save or share the files to the Files app.
  2. Add your layers (section 1). Tap + Add audio files, or Load demo takes to try it first.
    • Check each layer's type (vocal, acoustic guitar…). It decides how the layer is polished.
    • One layer is the timing master: its tempo sets the beat grid for timing correction and snapping. It starts as the first layer you add. Tap make timing master on another layer to switch (polished layers then need re-polishing). Choose your best-timed rhythm part (strummed guitar, drums, bass). Vocals don't give a clear beat.
  3. Listen. Press ▶ Play in the bar at the top (it stays there as you scroll). Use the mixer's M (mute) and S (solo), or ▶ Play this layer on a card.
  4. Arrange, if needed (section 2). Tidy and rearrange before polishing.
    • Move tool: tap a part to select it, drag to move it. Select tool: drag across a lane to select a section.
    • Then use Split (at the playhead), Cut, Copy, Paste (at the playhead), Duplicate or Delete. Undo always works.
    • Snap lines parts up with the beat or with another track's hits. Pinch to zoom, two fingers to scroll.
  5. Polish each layer (section 1, on each card).
    • Pick a preset in section 3 if you like, then type what you want in the layer's box: “warmer”, “less nasal”, “tighter”, “drier”. The box in section 3 does the same for the whole song (“make it more vintage”).
    • To fine-tune by hand, open Audio Adjustments on the card: EQ, dynamics, effects and mix sliders. Reset to preset undoes your changes.
    • Tap Polish this layer and listen. Not right? Make another request and Re-polish. The status shows when a layer needs re-polishing.
    • Tap 🎵 Detect notes to see the chords by bar (guitars, bass) or the melody (vocals), shown on the timeline too (toggle with 🎵 Notes). Re-detect after editing a layer.
  6. Balance the mix (section 4). Switch each layer between Raw and Polished while it plays to compare, and set volume and pan with the sliders.
    • Shortcuts: Polish all unpolished layers, All raw / All polished, and 🎵 Detect notes on all.
  7. Render the final mix (section 5). It uses exactly what the mixer is set to, then balances the song and sets streaming loudness. Compare it with the unprocessed mix, then download it.
  8. Download, at any stage. Choose the format once from ⬇ in the play bar: WAV (24-bit studio, 16-bit CD, 32-bit float), AIFF (Logic / Pro Tools) or MP3.
    • Each layer card: ⬇ Arranged, ⬇ Polished and ⬇ MIDI (detected notes).
    • Mixer: ⬇ All layers (ZIP) and ⬇ MIDI (all layers). Final mix: ⬇ Final mix, or ⬇ Final mix + all layers (ZIP).
    • Layer files all start at 0:00 and run the full song length, so they line up when dropped into GarageBand, Logic, Pro Tools or any other DAW.
    • On iPad, downloads go to Files → Downloads. Tap a ZIP to unzip it.
Good to know
  • Everything runs on your device. Your recordings are never uploaded, and nothing is generated: every sound is your own playing.
  • Editing never changes your original recordings. Order: arrange first, then polish.
  • Not sure what to type? See every phrase it understands. It also tells you when it can't do something.
  • No sound on iPad? Check the volume and that the iPad isn't muted (Control Centre), then press Play again.
  • Nothing is saved between visits yet, so download what you want to keep before closing the page.
  • On a keyboard: Space plays and stops, S splits, Ctrl/Cmd + Z undoes.
