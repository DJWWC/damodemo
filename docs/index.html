LayerPolish

Polish your own recordings one layer at a time: clean up noise, tighten timing, shape the tone. Then listen to the layers together, balance them, and render the song. Adjust it in plain English.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Your audio is never uploaded. Nothing is generated: every sound is yours.

1 Layers

One instrument or vocal per file, all recorded to the same tempo and starting at the same moment. Start each take with about a second of silence so the noise clean-up can learn your room. The first layer sets the timing.

WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC
No layers yet. Add your takes, or load the demo to try it.

2 Overall style

Applies to every layer. Each layer card also has its own request box.

Describe what you want or what's wrong, in your own words: “warmer”, “the vocal sounds nasal”, “guitar is too bright”, “slightly wider”. Name a layer to target it, otherwise it applies to everything. See every phrase it understands.

    3 Mixer

    Plays all layers together. Use M to mute and S to solo. Switch each layer between Raw and Polished while it plays. Volume and pan here are part of the final mix.

    Add layers to use the mixer.

    4 Final mix

    Renders what the mixer is set to: muted layers left out, each layer raw or polished as chosen, with your volume and pan. The song-level pass then makes room for the vocal, glues the mix and sets streaming loudness (−14 LUFS).

    Switch while playing to compare (loudness-matched)
